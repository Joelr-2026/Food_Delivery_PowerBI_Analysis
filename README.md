## Key Insights

### 1. Gurugram Cyber City generated the highest order volume
**Observation:** Gurugram Cyber City recorded the highest number of orders with 220 orders.

**Business Meaning:** Demand is strongest in this area compared with the other delivery locations.

**Recommendation:** Maintain sufficient delivery capacity and restaurant availability in Gurugram Cyber City, especially during peak periods.


### 2. Urban Tadka generated the highest delivered revenue
**Observation:** Urban Tadka generated the highest delivered revenue at approximately ₹173K.

**Business Meaning:** Urban Tadka is the strongest revenue-generating restaurant in the current dataset.

**Recommendation:** Maintain strong availability and delivery performance for this restaurant and study the factors behind its high revenue contribution.


### 3. UPI was the most used payment method
**Observation:** UPI was used for 529 orders, followed by COD with 508 orders.

**Business Meaning:** Digital payments are slightly more preferred than other payment options.

**Recommendation:** Keep the UPI payment experience fast and reliable while continuing to support COD because its usage is also high.


### 4. Average delivery time was approximately 48 minutes
**Observation:** The overall average delivery time was 47.92 minutes.

**Business Meaning:** Delivery operations generally take close to 48 minutes per order, which can directly affect customer experience.

**Recommendation:** Monitor preparation time, traffic conditions, and high-delay areas to identify opportunities to reduce delivery time.


### 5. Mayur Vihar and Saket had the highest average delivery time
**Observation:** Mayur Vihar and Saket had the highest average delivery time at approximately 49 minutes.

**Business Meaning:** These areas experience slightly slower delivery performance compared with the best-performing areas.

**Recommendation:** Review delivery routes, traffic impact, restaurant preparation time, and rider availability in these areas.


### 6. Cancellation rate was relatively low
**Observation:** The overall cancellation rate was 5.55%.

**Business Meaning:** Most orders were completed without cancellation, indicating generally stable order fulfillment.

**Recommendation:** Continue monitoring cancellations and investigate recurring operational patterns if the rate increases.


### 7. Average customer rating was moderate
**Observation:** The average customer rating was 3.01 out of 5.

**Business Meaning:** Customer satisfaction is moderate and leaves clear room for improvement.

**Recommendation:** Focus on faster delivery, order accuracy, restaurant quality, and customer service to improve ratings.


### 8. Overall business volume was substantial
**Observation:** The dataset contains 2,000 orders with approximately ₹1.80M in delivered revenue and an average order value of ₹979.13.

**Business Meaning:** The dataset represents meaningful order and revenue activity across multiple restaurants and delivery areas.

**Recommendation:** Prioritize high-performing restaurants and areas while using operational metrics such as delivery time and cancellations to improve overall performance.
